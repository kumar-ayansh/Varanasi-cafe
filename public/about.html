Our Story

A little bit of Varanasi, told one cup at a time.

Draped cotton ceilings, warm bulbs strung along wooden beams, and a menu built around the flavours of home — this is The Varanasi Story.

Chapter I — Where It Began

Cooking is love made visible

The Varanasi Story began as a tribute to ghat-side chai stalls and home kitchens — a place to slow down over adrak chai, wood-fired pizza, or a bowl of steaming maggie with people who matter. Every corner of the cafe was shaped around that one idea: a room that feels like an evening by the ghats, wherever you actually are.

🪔

We're entirely vegetarian, and everything is cooked to order — nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for you. Walk in for the room, or let us bring the story to you with order-ahead pickup or a reserved table for an evening that isn't rushed.
